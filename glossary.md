# CRA Glossary

Terms alphabetized. (Ch N) = chapter file where the term is explained in context; Art/Annex = legal basis.

**Actively exploited vulnerability** — a vulnerability for which there is reliable evidence that a malicious actor has exploited it in a system without the owner's permission; triggers the 24h reporting clock (Art 3(42), ch04).
**Authorised representative** — Union-established person with written mandate from a non-EU manufacturer to act on its behalf for specified tasks (Art 18, ch06).
**CE marking** — mark indicating conformity of the PDE and the manufacturer's processes with Annex I + other applicable Union harmonisation legislation; affixed only after conformity assessment + DoC (Art 3(31), Art 29–30, ch05).
**Component** — software or hardware intended for integration into an electronic information system; separately placed components are in scope (Art 3(6), ch01).
**Coordinated vulnerability disclosure (CVD)** — the manufacturer's enforced policy for receiving/handling externally reported vulnerabilities (Annex I II(5), Art 13(19), ch03/ch04).
**Conformity assessment** — process verifying Annex I requirements are met; routes: module A (internal control), B+C (EU-type examination + production conformity), H (full quality assurance) (Art 27, Art 32, ch05).
**Consumer** — natural person acting outside trade/business/profession (Art 3(18)).
**Critical product with digital elements** — Annex IV category (HSMs/security boxes, smart meter gateways, smartcards/secure elements); needs EUCC where a scheme exists, else Class II routes (Art 8, ch01/ch05).
**Cybersecurity risk** — potential for loss/disruption from an incident = magnitude × likelihood (Art 3(37), ch02).
**Data minimisation** — Annex I Part I(2)(g): process only data adequate, relevant and necessary for the intended purpose.
**Distributor** — supply-chain actor (not manufacturer/importer) making a PDE available on the EU market without affecting its properties; due-care + verification + escalation duties (Art 3(17), Art 20, ch06).
**Early warning notification** — first CRA report within 24h of awareness of an actively exploited vulnerability or severe incident (Art 14(2)(a)/(4)(a), ch04).
**Economic operator** — manufacturer, authorised representative, importer, distributor, or other person with CRA duties for making a PDE available (Art 3(12), ch06).
**Exploitable vulnerability** — vulnerability that could be effectively used by an adversary under practical operational conditions; release gate per Annex I Part I(2)(a) (Art 3(41)).
**Harmonised standard** — European standard whose OJEU publication gives presumption of conformity for the requirements it covers, when correctly applied (Art 3(36), Art 27, ch05).
**Important product with digital elements** — Annex III category; Class I or Class II; drives notified-body route unless harmonised standards fully apply (Art 7, ch01/ch05).
**Incident having an impact on the security of the PDE** — incident negatively affecting (or capable of) the product's protection of availability/authenticity/integrity/confidentiality of data or functions (Art 3(44), ch04).
**Intended purpose** — use specified in manufacturer's instructions, promotional/sales materials and technical documentation; defines obligation surface (Art 3(23), ch08).
**Importer** — Union-established person placing on the market a PDE bearing a non-EU person's name/trademark; verification + blocking + escalation duties (Art 3(16), Art 19, ch06).
**Making available on the market** — supply for distribution/use on the EU market in commercial activity, paid or free (Art 3(22)).
**Market surveillance authority (MSA)** — national authority enforcing CRA; may demand access to design/dev/vulnerability-handling documentation on reasoned request (Art 3(33), Art 52–53, ch07).
**Micro/small/medium enterprise** — per Recommendation 2003/361/EC; limited fine relief only (Art 3(19), Art 64(10)).
**Near miss** — incident that could have caused harm but didn't (DORA def., Art 3(45)).
**Notified body / notifying authority** — conformity-assessment body designated by a Member State to run B/C/H assessments; the designating national authority (Art 3(29), Ch IV).
**Open-source software steward** — legal person systematically supporting development of specific OSS products and ensuring their viability; policy + cooperation duties, fine-exempt (Art 3(14), Art 24, ch06).
**Placing on the market** — first making available of a PDE on the Union market (Art 3(21)).
**Product with digital elements (PDE)** — software or hardware product and its remote data processing solutions, placed on the market separately; the CRA's regulated object (Art 3(1), ch01).
**Reasonably foreseeable use / misuse** — likely uses/misuses from normal human behaviour or system interaction, even if not documented; part of the risk-assessment scope (Art 3(24)–(25)).
**Recall / withdrawal** — forcing back / removing a product already on the market (Art 3(49)–(50), via Reg. 2019/1020).
**Remote data processing** — cloud/SaaS processing designed by the manufacturer, absence of which prevents a product function; in scope as part of the PDE (Art 3(2)).
**SBOM (software bill of materials)** — formal record of software components + supply-chain relationships; required in common machine-readable format, ≥ top-level dependencies (Art 3(39), Annex I II(1)).
**Security update** — change addressing a security issue; must be free (standard products), disseminated without delay, retained ≥10 years (Annex I II(2)/(8), Art 13(9)).
**Significant cybersecurity risk** — high likelihood of incident with severe negative impact; the threshold triggering importer/distributor MSA escalation (Art 3(38), ch06).
**Single reporting platform** — EU-wide portal for CRA notifications to CSIRT coordinator + ENISA (Art 16, ch04).
**Substantial modification** — post-market change affecting Annex I Part I compliance or the assessed intended purpose; re-triggers full manufacturer duties (Art 3(30), ch06/ch07).
**Support period** — window in which vulnerability handling must be effective; ≥5 years minimum (or expected use time if shorter) (Art 3(20), Art 13(8)).
**Technical documentation** — the conformity dossier: product description, design/development info, risk assessment, standards used, assessment evidence; all versions reflected (Art 31, Annex VII, ch05).
**Vulnerability** — weakness/susceptibility/flaw exploitable by a cyber threat (Art 3(40)).
